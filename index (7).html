Who We Are

A Small Centre with a Big Purpose

Nexus Study Centre, based in Fontainebleau, Randburg, is a small, dynamic learning environment supporting learners from senior primary through to IGCSE and A-Level studies. The centre offers both the Cambridge International and CAPS curricula, allowing flexible academic pathways suited to each learner's goals and abilities.

With eight years of Cambridge and CAPS educational experience, Nexus provides personalised academic support, small-group instruction, and a nurturing environment that caters to diverse learning styles and neurotypes. Our small class sizes allow tutors to give each learner the attention they need, ensuring that students develop a strong understanding of their subjects while feeling confident to ask questions and engage with challenging academic material.

At Nexus, we believe meaningful learning happens when students feel supported, understood, and encouraged to think independently.

Our Values

The Principles That Guide Us

Every decision we make is guided by what is best for the learner — academically, emotionally, and developmentally.

🌱

Personalisation

No two learners are the same. We tailor our approach to each student's unique needs, goals, and learning style.

💪

Confidence Building

We prioritise building self-assurance alongside academic skill, helping learners feel capable and empowered.

🧩

Inclusion

Our environment welcomes diverse learning styles and neurotypes, ensuring every learner finds their place.

🔍

Critical Thinking
